Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

First Baptist Church of Capac respects your privacy. This page tells you what information this website collects and what we do with it.

Contact form

When you use the form on our Contact page, we get your name, email address, and message. We use this only to answer you and to pray for any prayer requests you send. We do not sell, rent, or share it with anyone. The form is handled by Formspree, which sends your message to our church email.

Online giving

Online gifts are made through Tithe.ly. Tithe.ly collects and protects your payment details. We never see or store your card or bank numbers. We may see your name, email, and gift amount so we can keep giving records and send giving statements.

Analytics and cookies

This website does not use analytics or tracking tools, and it does not set its own cookies.

Other services on this site

Some pages show content from other companies: Facebook (sermon videos and our page feed), Google Maps (the map on our Contact page), and Google Fonts (the text style). Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. These companies may receive your IP address and may set their own cookies, as their own privacy policies explain.

Children

This website is not meant for children under 13 to send us information. If you think a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
